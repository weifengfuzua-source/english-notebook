# 英语一精读 02｜Quiet Quitting

## 文章主线

文章质疑：quiet quitting 到底是不是一个真实的新劳动趋势？

> TikTok 上出现 quiet quitting → 媒体包装成新的劳动趋势 → 作者认为轻微的工作疏离早就存在 → 媒体把高离职率解释成“年轻人不想工作” → 数据表明很多人只是换工作，劳动力、就业岗位和总工时还在增加 → 职场热词在统计意义上未必准确 → 流行反映了真实而难以表达的工作不满 → 趋势可能是假的，但新词的流行是值得研究的文化现象。

统计事实可能不支持“quiet quitting 是新趋势”，但它的流行反映了真实的社会情绪。

## 原文

The hottest labor narrative right now is that everybody’s “[[p:quiet quitting]]”. Starting this summer, popular videos on TikTok with millions of views have used the term to refer to the art of having a job without letting it take over your life. The term crawled out of that social-media petri dish into the mainstream-media landscape.

Statistically speaking, quiet quitting is not actually a thing. Or, at least, it is not a new thing. As a workplace phenomenon, workers’ mild disengagement is about as [[v:novel]] as lunch breaks, and bleary-eyed colleagues stopping by your workstation to mutter. Admittedly, quiet quitting would seem to solve a major contemporary labor mystery. Labor productivity is falling after it [[v:surged]] in the first year of the pandemic. The best explanation for this decline, however, is not a sudden outbreak of TikTok-transmitted laziness.

The term has [[p:taken off]] in part because burned-out or bored workers are simply desperate for a fresh vocabulary to describe their feelings. Quiet quitting sounds to some like worker empowerment. But realistically, the term is [[s:more likely to]] [[e:validate]] managers who think that their employees are [[v:slackers]] [[s:than to]] help ordinary workers [[v:reclaim]] their soul. It offers a convenient explanation for seemingly lazy workers. Complex questions such as “Am I running my team effectively?” can [[p:be reduced to]] the confident [[v:diagnosis]] that young people just don’t want to work.

For some reason, news media seem eager to confirm these managers’ worst fears. Earlier this year, several outlets tried to convince Americans that “no one wants to work”, [[s:even as]] the economy added hundreds of thousands of jobs each month. The best [[v:evidence]] they had wasn’t [[s:evidence at all, but rather a misunderstanding]] of government data. As the official “quits rate” [[p:surged to all-time highs]] and the phrase Great Resignation [[p:made its way into headlines]], some commentators perceived an overall [[v:disinclination]] to work. But most people weren’t quitting to retire; they were quitting to take a new job. The [[e:labor force]], total jobs, and [[e:hours worked]] increased throughout a period of time when news organizations were telling everybody that work was dead. It was weird.

The popularity of these [[v:empty-calorie]] labor trends suggests a [[v:divergence]] between statistical and deeper emotional truth. Put another way, these [[v:quasi-trends]] are bad-vibe [[v:vehicles]]—[[p:delivery mechanisms]] for [[v:inexpressible]] negative ideas about the world that demand the news-headline treatment. When people are looking for permission to feel their [[v:unnameable]] bad feelings, they’re satisfied when [[v:cheeky]] TikTok accounts or [[v:trend-chasing]] journalists give it to them. Quiet quitting is a bit of novel nonsense that might [[p:stand in for]] [[v:chronic]] labor issues such as the [[v:underrepresentation]] of unions or a profound American pressure to be [[v:careerist]].

When a phrase [[p:takes off]], it’s often because the new words fill a space of uncertainty, like the coining of a new diagnosis. If quiet quitting is fake, the popularity of anti-work [[v:neologisms]] is its own [[p:data point]] that deserves to be [[p:taken seriously]] [[p:as a cultural phenomenon]].

## 错题复盘

作答：6 C ✅｜7 A → D ❌｜8 B ✅｜9 A ✅｜10 D ✅。成绩：4/5。

唯一错题为第 7 题：The term “quiet quitting” may...

正确答案：offer an excuse for managerial ineffectiveness，为管理不善提供一个借口。

> But realistically, the term is more likely to validate managers who think that their employees are slackers than to help ordinary workers reclaim their soul.

> It offers a convenient explanation for seemingly lazy workers.

原本经理应该问：“Am I running my team effectively?”；标签出现后却被简化成“young people just don’t want to work”。逻辑是：不是我管理差，是年轻人懒。

`convenient explanation ≈ excuse`；`poor management ≈ managerial ineffectiveness`。

不是文章完全没看懂，而是抽象动词没理解准确，后面的角色关系一起串线。最开始没认出 validate managers，人物关系开始漂移。

## 重点词汇与熟词僻义

validate：常见义验证；本文为证实某人的看法、给观点背书。

slacker：懒散的人、混日子的人、摸鱼的人。

reclaim：重新夺回、找回；reclaim their soul = 找回自我、找回被工作吞掉的生活。

diagnosis：本义诊断；抽象义判断、结论；the diagnosis that... = “……”这一判断。

claim：说法、主张、断言；不要机械翻成“声明”。

evidence：证据、依据；strong evidence = 有力证据；little evidence = 几乎没有；a little evidence = 有一点。

surge：激增、飙升；quits rate surged = 离职率飙升。

inclination：倾向、意愿。

disinclination：不愿意、缺乏意愿；dis- 表示否定；disinclination to work = 不愿工作的倾向。

calorie：卡路里、热量。

empty-calorie：原指热量高但营养少；本文比喻看起来热闹但实际解释力很低；empty-calorie labor trends = “没什么营养”的职场流行概念。

divergence：分歧、背离、分叉；a divergence between A and B = A 和 B 之间存在背离。

quasi-：准……、所谓的、类似但并不完全是；quasi-trends = 所谓的趋势、似是而非的趋势。

vehicle：本义车辆；抽象义载体、媒介；a vehicle for feelings = 情绪的载体。

inexpressible：难以表达的、难以言表的。

unnameable：难以命名的、难以准确说清的。

cheeky：俏皮大胆的、带点调侃或冒犯意味的。

trend-chasing：追热点的、追逐潮流的；trend-chasing journalists = 追热点的记者。

novel：名词小说；形容词新颖的、新出现的；novel nonsense = 新潮但没多少实质的说法、新式噱头。

chronic：长期的、慢性的；上一篇 chronic stress = 慢性压力；本文 chronic labor issues = 长期存在的劳工问题。

underrepresentation：代表性不足；under- = 不足，representation = 代表性；underrepresentation of unions = 工会代表性不足。

careerist：事业至上的、过度追求职业晋升的人；pressure to be careerist = 以事业成功为中心的社会压力。

neologism：新词、新造词、新表达；anti-work neologisms = 反工作类的新词。

## 高价值词块

quiet quitting：“安静离职、静默离职”；人还在工作，但不再让工作占据全部生活、不再额外投入过多；不是真正辞职。

all-time high：历史最高点、历史最高水平。

labor force：劳动力、劳动力人口；force 类似 police force（警察队伍）。

hours worked：工作时长、总工时。

statistical truth：统计数据层面的事实。

emotional truth：情绪体验层面的真实。

delivery mechanism：传递机制、输送渠道。

data point：字面数据点；本文为值得观察的事实、证据点；its popularity is its own data point = 它的流行本身就是一个值得观察的数据点。

validate someone's view：给某人的观点背书。

blame A for B：因 B 责怪 A、把 B 归咎于 A。

be reduced to：被简化为、被归结为。

the claim / diagnosis that...：“……”这一说法、判断。

surge to all-time highs：飙升至历史最高水平。

make one's way into headlines：登上新闻头条、进入公众视野。

disinclination to do：不愿做某事。

throughout a period：在整个时期内。

take off：迅速流行、走红；a phrase takes off 不是“起飞”。

stand in for：代替、充当……的替代品；新潮标签代替对复杂、长期劳工问题的真正讨论。

coin a term / phrase：创造一个新词、新说法。

take sth seriously：认真对待某事。

as a cultural phenomenon：作为一种文化现象。

## 可迁移句式

be more likely to A than to B：与其说 B，不如说更可能 A；比较两个可能性。

> But realistically, the term is more likely to validate managers who think that their employees are slackers than to help ordinary workers reclaim their soul.

名词 + that 从句：后面的 that 从句解释前面名词的具体内容；the diagnosis that... = “……”这一判断；同类有 the idea that、the belief that、the claim that、the fact that。

> Complex questions such as “Am I running my team effectively?” can be reduced to the confident diagnosis that young people just don’t want to work.

not A at all, but rather B：根本不是 A，而其实是 B；强否定加修正。

> The best evidence they had wasn’t evidence at all, but rather a misunderstanding of government data.

no less A than B：A 并不比 B 少、弱；不要看见 less 就翻成“更少”。

总结中的例句：younger workers were no less willing to work than older generations。照片原文未出现该句；保留为学习材料例句，不计入文章原句或原文出现次数。

even as：即使、尽管 B 正在发生，A 仍然……；常用于两个趋势相反。

> Earlier this year, several outlets tried to convince Americans that “no one wants to work”, even as the economy added hundreds of thousands of jobs each month.

抽象名词比喻：vehicle = 情绪载体；delivery mechanism = 情绪传播机制；empty-calorie trend = “没营养”的流行概念；不能逐词硬翻。

## 全文重要逻辑关系

> 统计事实 ≠ 情绪事实。

作者并不是说 quiet quitting 是假的，所以完全没意义。作为真正的新劳动趋势，它可能站不住脚；但能爆火，说明人们有真实而难以表达的负面工作情绪。

即使趋势是假的，热词的流行也是真的。“流行本身就是数据”是全文最后的落点。

## 本篇暴露的新薄弱点

1. 固定词组容易逐词拆坏。

- labor force：曾误成“强迫劳动”。
- hours worked：曾误成“小时工”。
- take off：曾误成“被取代”。
- validate：最开始没认出 validate managers 的语境义，导致人物关系漂移。

遇到每个词都认识但拼起来很怪时，先问：它是不是一个固定搭配？

2. 抽象义比字面义更容易卡住：vehicle、validate、diagnosis、data point、novel。
3. 陌生词后容易补剧情：曾把 labor force、hours worked、work was dead 拼成“黑心公司、强迫劳动、好工作消失”，这些内容原文都没有。问自己：“这是原文给我的信息，还是我自己脑补出来的？”
4. 句法主干不是主要短板；that 从句、who 从句、比较结构、not A but B、时间结构基本能抓住。重点是高频词抽象义、固定词块和逻辑关系。

## 最值得背的表达

1. validate someone's view：给某人的观点背书。
2. be reduced to：被简化为。
3. not A at all, but rather B：根本不是 A，而其实是 B。
4. surge to all-time highs：飙升至历史最高水平。
5. make its way into headlines：登上新闻头条。
6. labor force：劳动力人口。
7. divergence between A and B：A 与 B 之间的背离。
8. vehicle for...：……的载体。
9. stand in for：代替。
10. take off：迅速走红。

> 熟词不要只认字面义；词组翻得怪时先怀疑固定搭配；原文没说的东西不要自己补。
